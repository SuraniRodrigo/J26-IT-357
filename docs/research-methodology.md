# Research methodology

## Demand forecasting

Research notebooks and experiments should document preprocessing, feature engineering, training strategy, validation windows, and evaluation metrics.

## Supply disruption risk

Use observed supplier and vendor fields only when available. Do not fabricate missing identifiers or transformed scores without explicit documentation.

## Inventory optimization

Inventory optimization should prioritize operational KPIs such as service level, stockout rate, holding cost, and replenishment frequency rather than raw classification accuracy.

## Production scheduling

Production optimization should use material availability, capacity, machine availability, and disruption-aware constraints, and should support self-healing rescheduling when constraints change.
